! VLANs on the NM-16ESW switch module are not part of the running-config.
! They live in vlan.dat, and in GNS3 (Dynamips) they are lost every time the
! project is closed. Paste this on SW1 and SW2 at the privileged EXEC prompt
! after every start; the ports rejoin their VLANs by themselves.
!
! Part 1 (one switch) used only VLAN 10 and 20.
! Part 2 added VLAN 99. Part 3 added VLAN 30.

vlan database
vlan 10 name SALES
vlan 20 name ENG
vlan 30 name MGMT
vlan 99 name NATIVE
exit
